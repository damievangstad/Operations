# Vangstad Creamery — Event Field SOP (sample)

This is a placeholder SOP used for testing. In Phase 2 these sections are replaced by your real documents synced from Google Drive.

## Arrival and setup
Arrive at the venue at least 60 minutes before service time. Park the cart on a level, shaded surface within reach of the assigned power outlet. Confirm the serving location with the event contact before unloading. Wipe down all surfaces, set out the menu board, and place the handwashing station within arm's reach of the serving window.

## Priming the soft-serve machine
Plug the machine in and let it run on STANDBY for 10 minutes to cool. Pour chilled mix into the hopper up to the fill line — never above it. Switch from STANDBY to FREEZE and wait until the ready light turns green, usually 8 to 12 minutes. Pull a small test serving and discard it before serving guests. If the mix looks runny after 15 minutes, the machine is not cold enough — leave it on FREEZE longer, do not add more mix.

## Serving guidelines
Standard portion is a 5 to 6 ounce swirl in a cake cone or cup. Use a fresh scoop or spoon for each topping; never reuse between containers. For private events, serve in the order guests arrive. For public events, keep the line single-file and call out flavors that have run low. Average serving pace is about 60 to 80 servings per hour per server.

## Mix quantity planning
Plan roughly one gallon of mix per 25 guests for a two-hour event. Always bring one extra spare gallon beyond the estimate. For events over 150 guests, bring a second backup machine if available.

## Handling allergies and dietary requests
Tell guests the standard mix contains milk and may contain traces of nuts. Keep the dairy-free sorbet in a separate sealed container with its own dedicated scoop. If a guest asks about an ingredient you are unsure of, do not guess — tell them you will confirm and contact your manager.

## Cleanup and breakdown
Switch the machine to STANDBY, then drain remaining mix into the waste container — do not pour mix on the ground or down a storm drain. Run the rinse cycle twice. Wipe all surfaces, bag all trash, and leave the area cleaner than you found it. Photograph the cart and the serving area before leaving and send the photos to the team chat.

## Refunds and unhappy customers
At public events where guests pay per serving, you may issue a refund of a single serving at your discretion if a guest is unhappy. For anything larger than one serving, or any dispute about the event itself, do not promise anything — apologize, take the guest's name and contact, and escalate to your manager.

## Payment handling
For paid public events, use the assigned card reader and the Vangstad payment account only. Do not accept payment to a personal account. Cash should be counted and recorded on the event sheet at the end of the shift, then handed to the manager.

## Weather and safety
In rain, move the cart under cover or the provided tent; never operate electrical equipment in standing water. In heat above 95F, take a paced break every hour and keep water on hand. If severe weather threatens, pause service and contact your manager for a go or no-go decision.
